# RecallRadar terms of use

_Last updated: 15 November 2026. Draft for the app owner to review (ideally with legal advice) before publishing._

1. **What it is.** RecallRadar is a decision-support tool. It matches public FDA recall data to the device list you provide and shows how sure each match is.
2. **Not medical or regulatory advice.** It does not replace the manufacturer's recall notice, your clinical engineering procedures, or your regulatory obligations. A **likely** match always needs a person to check it, and a missing match is not proof that a device is safe.
3. **Data accuracy.** Results depend on the FDA's public data (openFDA), on the quality of your device list, and on how recent the saved check is. The app shows when the check was made and warns when lookups failed.
4. **Your responsibility.** You decide which tasks to create and whom to assign. Check created tasks and pages before acting on them.
5. **Your data.** You keep your data. Do not enter patient data or other personal health information.
